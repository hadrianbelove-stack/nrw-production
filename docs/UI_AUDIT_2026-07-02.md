# NRW UI Audit — July 2, 2026

**Scope:** the live production site (desktop web, mobile web) and the tvOS app (Release build, Apple TV simulator), audited as real users see them.
**Method:** 70 screenshots covering every view and state (`screenshots/ui-audit-2026-07-02/`), then 9 specialist design reviewers (first impression, typography, layout, color/accessibility, interaction, detail views, 10-foot TV, consistency, performance-as-UX). 103 raw findings were merged to 74; every high/medium finding was then re-verified by an adversarial checker that re-read the screenshots, re-checked every file:line citation, recomputed every contrast ratio, and checked whether in-flight work already fixes it. **47 findings survived; 4 were refuted.** Full evidence: `screenshots/ui-audit-2026-07-02/results.json`.

**Timing note:** mid-audit, the detail-density redesign was committed by concurrent sessions (5da68cc0, 2e6828b0, c0ef4b2c) and publish.yml is deploying it. All 47 findings were verified against the **current** code — none of them are fixed by those commits.

**No code was changed for this audit.** Everything below is a recommendation.

## Top 10 priorities

Ranked by impact for a browsing-first poster wall, weighed against effort.

1. **Give the wall its screen back.** Desktop keeps ~1/3 of every scrolled frame as pinned chrome (masthead + control bar + date strip); tvOS pins ~36%. Collapse the wordmark/slogan after the first scroll on both — keep only the control bar sticky. First paint keeps the full brand moment; browsing gets ~130px back. *(F1, F2 — medium effort)*
2. **Fix the two broken detail moments.** The desktop lightbox truncates film titles ("Sun Ra: Do the I…") while half the panel sits empty, and at ≤1100px the poster crops to a landscape slab with the info squeezed to a sliver. Both are small CSS fixes. *(F7, F8)*
3. **Raise tvOS type to 10-foot sizes.** The signature streaming-frame band renders service names at 15px and "NOW STREAMING" at 8px; fest names, date pills, and score chips run 10–14px. Apple's floor is ~23pt. The gallery-label idea is right — it just can't be read from a couch. *(F27)*
4. **Make the slop control self-teaching, and give filtered states an exit.** The 3-state slop toggle looks identical to the binary toggles beside it; "slop" is never defined; active filters show no count and no inline clear. Tick marks in the track, a title/aria explainer, and a small "HORROR — 1 film · Clear ✕" status chip fix the whole cluster. *(F13, F16)*
5. **The speed bundle (all trivial/small).** Drop the `?t=Date.now()` cache-buster (every visit re-downloads ~525 KB that GitHub Pages already knows how to cache), preload data.json from the HTML, stop deleting the tvOS cache on every launch, and give desktop a skeleton wall instead of a blank black page. Together these transform time-to-first-poster. *(F40–F44)*
6. **Set a mobile text floor.** Pre-order dates at 4.8px, prices at 8.8px, filter labels at 8.3px — functional text rendering as decorative specks. Floor it at 9–10px; where the 3-col grid can't afford that, drop the text and let color carry it to the detail sheet. *(F30)*
7. **One score vocabulary.** Every surface shows a different pair of scores in a different style; TV shows bare colored numbers with no key; Letterboxd renders three different ways and the star-rounding inflates 3.5 to 4★ on desktop and TV — an honesty problem for a curation brand. Define one score-chip spec, share it, and show the real 3.5. *(F19, F20)*
8. **Decide the sparse-view layout once.** A genre or pre-order filter often yields one left-anchored card under two full-width banners — 80–90% chrome and void, worst on TV. Either center sparse groups or switch filtered views to a packed grid with slim date separators. *(F5, F6)*
9. **One consistency pass, driven by the style guide.** STYLE_GUIDE.md still says Selects are crimson; fests use two different golds; Amazon's VOD button is Prime-blue on desktop/TV and orange on mobile; mobile's restoration badge is a green that exists nowhere else; the brand lockup renders three different ways. Update the guide to the shipped identity, move the tokens into shared-config, and fix the ~6 outliers. *(F21–F26)*
10. **Browser history integration.** Back exits the site instead of closing the lightbox/poster view (on iOS the swipe-back gesture — the dominant dismissal — leaves the site), and `?m=` deep links persist after close. ~15 lines per codebase. *(F45)*

**Quick-wins shelf (all trivial, could ship in one sitting):** purple PRE-ORDER label tint (#a78bfa — current fails WCAG at 3.5:1), tvOS inactive label alpha 0.45→0.75, `flex:0 0 auto` on lightbox score chips, remove the 1500px masthead cap at 1920 (misaligned left rail), `clamp()` the wordmark (clips at 770–860px), "FESTS AVAILABLE NOW" banner wording, cache-buster removal, data.json preload.

## All confirmed findings

Severity shown is post-verification. Effort: trivial (a few lines) / small (an hour-ish) / medium (a real task). Evidence citations were all re-verified against the current tree.

### A. Screen real estate & the wall

- **F1 · HIGH · desktop · medium — Sticky chrome leaves ~1 poster row visible at 1440×900.** ~300–310px (~34%) of every scrolled frame is pinned masthead + control bar + date strip (styles.css:56–65, 399–415; desktop-02). Fix: collapse wordmark/slogan past ~100px scroll; `--header-height` is already dynamic (app.js:228) so the date strip follows.
- **F2 · MEDIUM · tvOS · medium — Pinned header spends ~36% of the TV screen.** 90px wordmark + slogan + control bar + date strip before the first poster pixel, permanently (HomeScreen.tvos.js:1437; tv-01/tv-11). Fix: recede wordmark+slogan once focus passes row 1, restore on scroll-back.
- **F3 · MEDIUM · desktop · trivial — Misaligned left rail at 1920.** Masthead/control bar capped at 1500px centered while the wall runs full-bleed (styles.css:1931 vs 274–280; desktop-22) — contradicts the fill-the-width rule. Fix: remove or match the cap.
- **F4 · MEDIUM · desktop · trivial — Wordmark clips at 770–860px.** "THE NEW RELEASE WAL" (desktop-25); nowrap 3.3rem with fixes only ≤768px (styles.css:70–72, 97–99). Fix: `font-size: clamp(2rem, 5.2vw, 3.3rem)`.
- **F5 · MEDIUM · cross · medium — Sparse filtered date groups read as broken.** One left-anchored card under full-width banners; ~80–90% chrome/void (tv-18, desktop-09). Fix (decide once): center sparse groups, or pack filtered views into a continuous grid with slim date separators.
- **F6 · MEDIUM · tvOS · medium — Genre filtering on TV collapses into near-empty shelves.** Same root cause as F5 at the surface where the wall identity matters most (tv-18). Coordinate the fix with F5.

### B. Detail views

- **F7 · HIGH · desktop · small — Lightbox title hard-truncates to one line.** 26-char titles become "Sun Ra: Do the I…" while the panel's lower half sits empty (styles.css:1409–1420; desktop-16). Mobile and tvOS both wrap 2 lines. Fix: `-webkit-line-clamp:2`, move the SELECT badge to the sub-row if it crowds.
- **F8 · HIGH · desktop · small — ≤1100px stacked lightbox breaks.** Poster crops to a ~576×400 landscape slab (base width rule survives the media query, styles.css:1311–1318 vs 1895–1898) and info collapses to a 40vh sliver with no scroll cue (desktop-24). Fix: `width:auto; aspect-ratio:2/3` in the query + rebalance poster/info heights + a scroll affordance.
- **F9 · MEDIUM · tvOS · medium — Detail card: undersized synopsis over a ~40% dead zone.** 24pt body (HIG: 29pt) over a ~90-char measure, then ~470px of blank panel above the pinned footer on quote-less films (tv-24, MovieDetail.tvos.js:45, 1152–1156). Fix: 26–28pt, ~70ch measure, distribute content vertically; keep the anchored footer.
- **F10 · MEDIUM · desktop · trivial — Lightbox score chips stretch into half-empty pills.** Base `.score-badge{flex:1}` leaks into the lightbox override (styles.css:845–851 vs 1488–1500; desktop-16). Fix: `flex: 0 0 auto`.
- **F11 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Hero poster renders soft.** Lightbox (~1152×1728 device px) and mobile close-up are fed the w500 file — a ~2.3× upscale; tvOS already swaps to w780 (useMovieDetail.js:210–220). Fix: port the `getPosterUrl` pattern to both webs.
- **F12 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Detail metadata grammar drifts on four axes.** "Dir:" vs "Director:" vs "D:"; USA·Doc·Jul 2 vs Doc·Jul 2·USA; "84 min" vs "1h 24m"; distributor vs language on the closing line. Fix: write the one-paragraph canonical spec in STYLE_GUIDE, align all renderers, run /check-devices.
- **F13 — merged into F16/F19 numbering below.** *(see Filter system and Scores)*

### C. Filter system & controls

- **F14 · MEDIUM · cross · medium — The 3-state slop toggle reads as a binary switch and "slop" is never defined.** Identical at rest to the binary toggles; label names the control, other states name the result; cycling FREE→default passes through SLOP ONLY; no title/aria anywhere (styles.css:190–218; app.js:179–183; HomeScreen.tvos.js:286–297). Fix: three tick marks in the track, click-thirds jump on web, aria/tooltip copy defining slop.
- **F15 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Exclusive-group clearing is silent, and SLOP FILTER behaves as a view.** Activating any view silently flips off the previous one (worst on TV, away from focus); clicking SLOP with a genre active discards the genre entirely (app.js:259–269, 184). Fix: pulse the auto-cleared control's label; propose letting slop-free compose with genre views.
- **F16 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Active filters show no result count and no inline exit.** HORROR = one card, no "1 film," no clear without reopening the popover; slop-free changes the wall with no banner at all; TV search proves the pattern ("23 results") (desktop-12, tv-18, desktop-03). Fix: status chip — "HORROR — 1 film · Clear ✕".
- **F17 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Search silently bypasses the active view but leaves its toggle lit.** A query ignores every toggle/genre while the header still claims the scope (app.js:403–409; desktop-14). Fix: dim/disable the toggle group while searching + "Searching all films" microcopy.
- **F18 · LOW/POLISH · cross · trivial — Toggle mechanics polish.** Same slop state parks the thumb in different positions on desktop vs TV (all=left vs all=middle); FESTS is the only view whose banner never says its keyword ("AVAILABLE NOW"); white thumbs measure 1.9–2.2:1 on active tracks (labels carry the state, so cosmetic). Fixes: pick one thumb order, "FESTS AVAILABLE NOW", dark thumb on filled tracks.

### D. Identity, badges & scores

- **F19 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Score chips are unlabeled, mixed-scale, and different on every surface.** TV shows bare "4.0"/"7.8" colored chips with no key; desktop shows logo'd IMDb+LB; mobile shows solid unlogo'd RT+IMDb blocks via inline cssText (MovieCard.tvos.js:358–361; app.js:746–756; mobile.js:655–668). Fix: label TV chips ("IMDb 4.0"), then define ONE score-chip spec in the style guide and share it.
- **F20 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Letterboxd has three grammars and the rounding inflates 3.5→4★.** Desktop/tvOS `Math.round` to whole stars; mobile prints the honest "3.5" (app.js:65–68; mobile.js:980–983). Fix: one grammar everywhere — numeric "3.5" is cheapest and most honest.
- **F21 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Streaming-frame headers hard-slice platform names ("PBS DOCUME" / "PBS DOCU").** JS character slices — 10 chars on desktop, 8 on TV — on any service missing from the resolver map, while the column fits ~20 (app.js:705; MovieCard.tvos.js:216). Fix: delete the slices, add short display names to the shared map, CSS-ellipsis fallback.
- **F22 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Nothing signals human curation.** Only identity copy anywhere is the wordmark + slogan; no about/footer/contact; "★ NRW SELECT ★" is an initialism never established (index.html:44–45). Fix: a quiet ABOUT/ⓘ at the control bar's far edge opening a one-paragraph explainer that doubles as the badge/filter legend.
- **F23 · MEDIUM · cross · small — STYLE_GUIDE.md has drifted from the shipped identity.** Guide still says Selects=crimson; fest identity split between two golds (#f59e0b toggles/banners vs #FFD700 badges); pre-order purple, slop orange, and TV focus orange undocumented. Fix: one documentation pass + move category hexes into shared-config so surfaces can't disagree.
- **F24 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Amber/gold/orange collisions.** FESTS #f59e0b vs slop-only/Purchase #ff9500 = 1.02:1; the action genre strip is literally the Purchase hex; TV focus ring is also #ff9500. The "which view am I in" color code fails where it matters (desktop-04 vs desktop-07). Fix: give slop-only its own hue (gray-orange per the guide's slop gray), move action off #ff9500.
- **F25 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Amazon VOD button disagrees across surfaces.** Prime-blue on desktop/TV, orange on mobile — and the committed mobile code itself mixes both (styles.css:1830; mobile.css:1260). Fix: pick one (guide favors Purchase/Rent orange, keeping blue for actual Prime streaming) and normalize.
- **F26 · MEDIUM/LOW · cross · trivial — Token strays.** Mobile restoration badge is hardcoded green #4a7c3f (guide: antique gold #C8A951, used by desktop+TV) (mobile.js:646). Brand lockup renders three ways (gradient / solid-teal / white-with-teal-slogan). Fix: token the badge; pick the desktop gradient as canonical lockup.

### E. Legibility & accessibility

- **F27 · HIGH · tvOS · medium — Streaming-frame band and badges are far below 10-foot legibility.** Service name 15px, "NOW STREAMING" 8px, fest names 13px, date pills 11px, restoration badges 10px, score chips 14px (MovieCard.tvos.js:594, 601, 618, 636, 484, 541, 668); HIG floor ~23pt. Fix: ~72px band, 24–28px names, delete or enlarge the sub-line, 18–20px badge floor.
- **F28 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Mobile/TV captions are undersized and end-truncate, losing the pinned Genre·Nation contract.** Desktop implements the guide's pinned-suffix pattern; mobile is one 9.6px ellipsizing span ("Nick Mead · Music · U…"), TV leaves dangling separators (mobile.css:352–360; MovieCard.tvos.js:574–579). Fix: port the pinned-suffix pattern; ≥0.7rem mobile, 23–24pt TV.
- **F29 · MEDIUM · desktop · small — Caption hierarchy inversion.** The only text under a poster is the teal meta line at section-header scale (1.1rem/700) while the title is suppressed — style guide specs the reverse; text-light posters become unidentifiable. Fix: decide — drop meta to metadata scale, or restore the 1-line title; update the guide either way.
- **F30 · MEDIUM · mobile · small — Functional text at 4.8–8.8px.** Pre-order badge 4.8px, prices 8.8px, filter labels 8.3px (mobile.css:394, 503, 1095, 151–159). Fix: 9–10px floor for anything informational; shorten labels and let toggles distribute wider if the row overflows.
- **F31 · MEDIUM · cross · trivial — Two contrast failures.** Active PRE-ORDER purple label 3.5:1 on both webs (needs 4.5) — use #a78bfa for text, keep #7c3aed for fills. tvOS inactive toggle labels at 45% teal = 2.8:1 — match web's 0.75 alpha (one line).
- **F32 · MEDIUM · desktop · medium — Wall keyboard navigation and view toggles are invisible to assistive tech.** Grid selection is a CSS class, DOM focus never moves; toggles have role=button but no aria-pressed (app.js:1987–2001, 2016–2018). Fix: move real focus to the card's expand button; add aria-pressed + stateful labels.

### F. Mobile flows

- **F33 · MEDIUM · mobile · small — View dots: 6px unlabeled targets hiding the whole 3-mode system.** Below every touch standard, meanings unguessable (dots read as carousel pages), and switching views from the grid always jumps to movie #1 (mobile/index.html:73–76; mobile.css:278–292; mobile.js:1387–1397). Fix: ≥44px hit areas, glyphs or labels, open the first movie currently on screen.
- **F34 · MEDIUM · mobile · small — Poster view gives no cue that details exist; the sheet handle goes two places.** Trailer/watch actions live only in the hidden third view; handle-tap → grid but handle-drag → poster (mobile.js:1421–1439, 1495–1501). Fix: a "⌃ Details" lip on the poster view; one dismissal destination for the handle.

### G. tvOS specifics

- **F35 · MEDIUM · tvOS · small — Focused row-1 card occludes the date banner; scrolled rows leave poster slivers.** 1.12 focus scale blooms over the strip (focusManager.tvos.js:171; tv-08, tv-10). Fix: ~28–32px clearance under the strip + a fade/scrim under the control bar.
- **F36 · MEDIUM · tvOS · medium — Search results are unreadable behind the keyboard blur.** The hint promises "Results appear as you type"; the system keyboard blurs the live grid until commit (tv-20 vs tv-21; SearchScreen.tvos.js:146–152, 175). Fix: live count beside the query, or the Roku-style two-mode layout; short-term, reword the hint.
- **F37 · MEDIUM · tvOS · small — The app deletes its movie cache before every load.** Cold launch always blocks on the full ~523 KB fetch; the offline fallback can never fire at launch (useHomeScreen.js:65). Fix: delete that line — render cached wall instantly, refresh in place.

### H. Speed as UX

- **F38 · MEDIUM · cross · trivial — `?t=Date.now()` cache-buster defeats all caching on all three clients.** GitHub Pages already serves ETag + max-age=600 + gzip (measured 541 KB gz / 3.07 MB raw); every visit re-downloads everything (app.js:81; mobile.js:82; api.js:25–28). Fix: drop it; rely on ETag/304, or key on the date if staleness ever matters.
- **F39 · MEDIUM · desktop · small — Blank black page while data loads.** Desktop renders nothing until the fetch resolves; mobile has a spinner (index.html:103; app.js:78–115). Fix: skeleton wall — a date bar + 8–12 empty 2:3 boxes reusing the poster-fallback gradient.
- **F40 · MEDIUM · cross · trivial — data.json fetch starts last.** HTML→CSS→JS→then the biggest resource (no preload anywhere). Fix: `<link rel="preload" href="data.json" as="fetch" crossorigin>` in both heads (requires the cache-buster fix first so URLs match).
- **F41 · MEDIUM · mobile · small — 3-col grid downloads w500 (~78 KB) for 126px cells.** w342 is 46% smaller and retina-sharp; deep browsing costs ~52 MB vs ~28 MB on cellular (mobile.js:607, 618). Fix: swap /w500/→/w342/ in the grid renderer only.

### I. Navigation & search correctness

- **F42 · MEDIUM · cross · small — No browser-history integration.** Back exits the site instead of closing overlays; iOS swipe-back leaves the site from the poster view; `?m=` persists after close so refresh reopens the old movie. Fix: pushState/popstate on open/close, replaceState to strip `?m` (~15 lines per codebase).
- **F43 · MEDIUM · cross · small — Search matches different fields per surface.** Web matches 8 fields with accent folding; tvOS matches 4 with none — same query, different films; placeholder and empty-state copy each describe a third thing (shared-config.js:211–221 vs SearchScreen.tvos.js:54–63). Fix: port the shared matcher verbatim to TV (and iOS/Android/Roku); align the copy on "title, director, cast."
- **F44 · LOW · desktop · small — One arrow-key over-press past a button row silently switches movies.** Left/Right past a row's edge calls lightboxNav — the same keys mean focus-move or movie-nav depending on the row (app.js:1231–1250). Fix: no-op past edges; keep movie nav on the trailer row and chevrons.

## Minor findings (verified only for plausibility, not adversarially)

Copy/consistency: today's strip never says TODAY; mobile genre sheet is missing the Thriller chip; first caption separator loses its space ("Christine Turner· Documentary"); mobile search empty state is a bare "No movies found"; desktop prints literal "Unknown" as distributor; "Buy Ticket" vs "Buy Tickets" / "SHARE" vs "Share"; genre Clear always enabled; lightbox SELECT badge condition narrower than the wall's; NRW SELECT badge flips idiom (web pill vs TV strip).
Contrast polish: pull-quote attributions 3.7:1; white logos on light brand fills (Prime, Hoopla, Crackle) 2.4–3.4:1; legacy solid score-badge palette still in CSS.
TV polish: reel counter "1/54" unexplained/undersized; 68px edge insets vs Apple's 90px title-safe (unverifiable in simulator); genre chips clumped left over a still-lit control bar; focus/selection tells multiple color stories (orange ring / teal pills / a desktop *green* `.grid-selected` outline).
Perf polish: `loading="lazy"` on above-the-fold posters with no fetchpriority; no adjacent-poster prefetch when paging; data.json is pretty-printed (3.07 MB raw vs 2.29 MB minified); a wall-first payload split (149 KB vs 523 KB gz) is the bigger long-term lever.
UX: the "MORE (n more)" button interrupts the scroll every 60 posters and can split a date group mid-day; redundant ⛶ expand button dims every poster corner while the caption is a dead click zone.

## Refuted during verification

- "tvOS control-bar focus is sometimes invisible" and "d-pad moves produce no visible change on detail" — artifacts of how the simulator was driven during capture, not app bugs (focus was simply not where the driver assumed).
- "Committed detail fixes aren't deployed" — publish was in flight during the audit.
- "Mobile grid is missing the streaming-frame treatment" — it exists; the sampled screenshot area just had none.

## Caveats

- tvOS was audited in the simulator. Focus feel, d-pad ergonomics, and overscan/title-safe margins still need the on-device eyeball (known standing item).
- Screenshots predate the density-redesign deploy that landed mid-audit; findings were all verified against the current code, so none are stale.
