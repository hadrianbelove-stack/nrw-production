# Service Logos

Place streaming service logos here for the tvOS watch buttons.

## Required Files

- `amazon.png` - Amazon Prime Video logo (32x32 or larger)
- `apple-tv.png` - Apple TV logo (32x32 or larger)
- `netflix.png` - Netflix logo (32x32 or larger)
- `hulu.png` - Hulu logo (32x32 or larger)
- `max.png` - Max (HBO Max) logo (32x32 or larger)
- `disney-plus.png` - Disney+ logo (32x32 or larger)
- `peacock.png` - Peacock logo (32x32 or larger)
- `paramount-plus.png` - Paramount+ logo (32x32 or larger)
- `mubi.png` - MUBI logo (32x32 or larger)
- `criterion.png` - Criterion Channel logo (32x32 or larger)

## Notes

- Use PNG format with transparency
- Recommend @2x and @3x versions for high-DPI displays
- Keep logos simple and recognizable at small sizes
- Follow each service's brand guidelines for logo usage
