SEED EXAMPLES
=============

These are approved capsules showing the target voice and style.
Study the rhythm: sentence fragments, specific hooks, no throat-clearing.


El hombre de la luz (2025) -- Christian Márquez
Venezuelan sci-fi. Shot in the Andes. Follows a man desperate to alter his tragic past in a homemade time machine. Based on real-life [**Luis Zambrano**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Zambrano), self-taught inventor who built electric turbines and brought power to his remote town before the state. Won Best Sci-Fi Film at Milan Film Fest.

---

A Second Life (2026) -- Laurent Slama
Shot guerrilla-style over a 14-day sprint during the 2024 Paris Olympics — small cameras, backpacks, a tiny crew embedded in the actual Opening Ceremony crowds. A hearing-impaired woman (**Agathe Rousselle** of *Titane*) whose carefully managed life is upended by a free-spirited Californian (**Alex Lawther**).

---

Kylie: Tension Tour Live (2026) -- Steve Price
Concert film of **Kylie Minogue**'s 2025 Tension World Tour. Made in conjunction with the three-part Netflix doc *Kylie*. Includes the unexplained appearance of "Sinister Gumbies" during the "Better the Devil You Know" number.

---

Blue Scuti: Tetris Crasher (2025) -- Chris Moukarbel
Doc about **Willis Gibson**, the 13-year-old who became the first person to 'beat' *Tetris* — reaching its kill screen and crashing the game. Director **Chris Moukarbel** (*Gaga: Five Foot Two*) captures not just the viral moment but the tight-knit competitive *Tetris* community. Tribeca premiere.

---

Rich Flu (2024) -- Galder Gaztelu-Urrutia
Spanish sci-fi thriller from the director of *The Platform* (a surprise Netflix smash). A mysterious virus selectively targets the richest people on the planet. Premiered at Sitges.

---

The Ride Ahead (2024) -- Samuel Habib
**Samuel Habib** — living with GNAO1 Neurodevelopmental Disorder, cerebral palsy, epilepsy, and speech challenges — made and stars in this coming-of-age documentary about his own journey into independence. Two GoPros mounted on his wheelchair capture the world from his perspective. Most of the crew are disabled. Co-directed with his father **Dan Habib**.

---

Propeller One-Way Night Coach (2026) -- John Travolta
**John Travolta** makes his directorial debut adapting his own 1997 children's novel — a love letter to the golden age of propeller plane travel. The film is a family affair: Travolta narrates, his daughter **Ella Bleu Travolta** co-stars, his sister Margaret and brother Joey appear, and the film is dedicated to his late wife and son. Cannes premiere.

---

Still Single (2025) -- Jamal Burger
Profile of **Masaki Saito**, Toronto's sushi superstar and the first Canadian chef to earn two Michelin stars. Directors **Jamal Burger** and **Jukan Tateisi** delve into his exquisite craft and his 'sometimes unruly life' outside the kitchen. The title comes from Saito's Michelin acceptance speech: 'I am still single, I don't know why.'

---

Miss You, Love You (2026) -- Jim Rash
A darkly funny drama, *Miss You, Love You* centers on a sharp-tongued widow (**Allison Janney** from *I, Tonya* and *The West Wing*), who finds an unexpected connection when she's forced to plan her husband's funeral with her estranged son's assistant (**Andrew Rannells** from *Girls*, *The Book of Mormon*). Director **Jim Rash** was the Oscar-winning co-writer of Alexander Payne's *The Descendants*. Features Janney singing a Barry White song in church.

---

Król dopalaczy (2026) -- Pat Howl
Polish crime thriller inspired by the "dopalacze" craze that swept Poland. These legal psychoactive substances were sold through a loophole, making overnight millionaires before a 2018 ban shut it all down. Director **Pat Howl** captures the moment in this *Wolf of Wall Street*-esque rise and fall tale. Sold to Disney+.
