ANTI-EXAMPLES: DO NOT WRITE LIKE THIS
======================================

These are examples of BAD capsule writing. Study them to understand what to avoid.


BAD: Generic TMDB marketing copy
---------------------------------
"A young woman discovers she has extraordinary powers and must use them to save
her family from a dark threat. Along the way, she learns the true meaning of
courage and sacrifice."
WHY IT'S BAD: Pure plot summary. No editorial voice. No context. Could describe
any movie.

---

BAD: Overwritten critical praise
---------------------------------
"A breathtaking tour de force that announces director X as a major new voice in
cinema. This stunning, masterful achievement is a riveting, jaw-dropping
experience that will leave audiences spellbound."
WHY IT'S BAD: All superlatives, zero specifics. Doesn't tell you anything about
the actual movie. Uses every banned marketing word.

---

BAD: Stuffy academic criticism
-------------------------------
"This film explores the liminal spaces between identity and alienation in
post-industrial society, interrogating the viewer's assumptions about the nature
of belonging through a carefully constructed mise-en-scène that echoes the
European art cinema traditions of the 1960s."
WHY IT'S BAD: Pretentious, jargon-heavy, no personality. Film school paper, not
a movie calendar. Nobody talks like this.

---

BAD: Plot summary with adjectives
-----------------------------------
"Set in 1970s New York, this gripping drama follows a conflicted detective as he
navigates a web of corruption and betrayal. With powerful performances and a
thought-provoking script, this unforgettable film keeps you on the edge of your
seat until its shocking conclusion."
WHY IT'S BAD: Just a plot summary dressed up with banned adjectives. No mention
of the director's work, no festival context, no production history, nothing
specific or editorial.

---

BAD: Rhetorical question opener
---------------------------------
"What happens when a brilliant scientist pushes the boundaries of human knowledge
too far? This riveting thriller from acclaimed director X takes us on a journey
into the heart of darkness, where nothing is as it seems."
WHY IT'S BAD: Rhetorical questions are lazy. "Nothing is as it seems" is a
meaningless cliché. No actual information about the movie.
