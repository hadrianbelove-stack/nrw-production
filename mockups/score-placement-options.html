Quick Comparison

Option Scanability Weight +Lines Tradeoff
1. Inline w/ Title ★★★ Low 0 Can crowd long titles
2. Score Row ★★★ Medium +1 Clean but adds a row
3. Right of Title Row ★★★ Low 0 Date moves left, less prominent
4. In Meta Line ★★ Minimal 0 Scores can get lost in metadata
5. Score Bar Strip ★★★ Medium +1 Most "designed" feel
6. Beside Date ★★ Minimal 0 Compact but may feel cramped
7. Under Date ★★★ Low 0 Elegant, needs taller title row
8. Poster Overlay ★★★ Medium 0 Desktop only; poster is separate
9. Plain Text ★★ Minimal +1 Most understated, could be missed
