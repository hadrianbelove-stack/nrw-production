DESIGN NOTES

  • Option C — Diagonal corner ribbon. Classic "special edition" feel. Doesn't compete with score badges (bottom-left) or any future streaming badge (top-right). Festival name in solid gold bar.
  • Option D — Split bar. Most compact — both pieces of info in one bar. "V.SCREENING" in solid gold, festival name in translucent gold. Risk: tight space for long names, but abbreviations help.
  • Option E — Stacked double bar. Clearest hierarchy. Thin gold strip labels it as a screening, dark bar below gives the festival name breathing room. Slightly taller card footprint.
  • Option F — Top poster banner. "V.SCREENING" across the top of the poster image itself. Very visible — you can't miss it. Festival name in the bottom bar. Slightly covers the poster top edge.
  • Option G — Pill + inline text. Most minimal. No bar at all — festival name is just a gold text line between the title and director. Clean but less prominent. Pill badge on poster matches Netflix pattern exactly.
  • All options use "V.SCREENING" as the badge text.
  • All options show the festival name on the card without opening the lightbox.
