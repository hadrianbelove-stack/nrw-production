Button Style Options for Detail Screen

Option A: Solid Filled (Current Style)

Simple solid colors. Text labels. What we have now but refined.

Option B: Minimal Outline

Transparent background with colored borders. Clean, doesn't compete with poster.

Option C: Logo + Label (Subtle Background)

Logo images with text labels. Semi-transparent tinted backgrounds.

Option D: Compact Chips

Small pill-shaped chips. Dark backgrounds with brand-colored accents.

Option E: Logo-Only Squares

Icon/logo only. No text. Compact and clean. Relies on recognizable brand logos.

Option F: Gradient Glass

Subtle gradients with glass effect. More premium/modern feel.
