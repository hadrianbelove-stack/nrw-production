REDESIGNED
Trailer RT 61% Wiki
Just a compact info row. 3 grey blocks → one clean row.

Layout: Watch buttons stacked (each on its own line, full width, colored) → Info row (Trailer + RT + Wiki in compact grid).
Streaming and rent/buy are always on separate lines for clarity.

Color scheme (matching mobile/tvOS):
Trailer = red (#E50914) • Stream = service color (Netflix red, Max purple, Disney blue, etc.) • Purchase = orange (#ff9500) • Plex = yellow (#E5A00D) • RT/Wiki = glass with border

Title + date: Aligned on the same baseline.

Logos: In the mockup, service names are shown as text. In the real implementation, Amazon and Apple buttons will show their actual logo PNGs (same images already used on the card back).
